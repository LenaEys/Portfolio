Trip-Perturbation Device

Tackling the issue: how do you trigger a repeatable trip perturbation in the lab without relying on a person to manually pull the barrier?

Our design used a flip-up tripping barrier that could be triggered by either computer input or forceplate contact, giving researchers a consistent, repeatable perturbation instead of a hand-pulled one. I prepared the bill of materials, ran the torque calculations for the barrier's weight, wrote the Arduino code, and took the circuit from schematic through breadboard integration — troubleshooting with a multimeter across several design iterations. I also coordinated with outside manufacturers to keep parts on schedule and within tolerance, and delivered the full set of design documents and prototypes in 75% of the allotted timeframe.
