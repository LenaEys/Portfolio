UTESCA: Haptic Vision

Tackling the issue: how can a 2D digital interface be navigated and interacted with when converted to a tactile model?

As part of UTESCA's Haptic Vision project, I helped build a robotic solution that lets visually impaired users interact with digital platforms through touch. My role was converting flat 2D user interfaces into 3D models, using Python and Unity, so they could be integrated with the team's existing robotic prototypes. The finished product went on to win the 2026 Troost ILead Difference Maker Award, along with a $50,000 scholarship.
