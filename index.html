LE

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Toronto, currently in the PEY Co-op stream. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design and embedded systems — I've built a triggerable trip-perturbation device for neuromechanics research, run biomechanics experiments on footwear friction and torsion, and helped convert 2D interfaces into 3D models for a robotic platform built to help visually impaired users navigate digital tools.

Outside the lab, I compete on U of T's varsity cross country team and train middle-distance events with Laurel Creek Track Club — a provincial steeplechase title and a national bronze among the highlights. I hold a CSWA certification in SolidWorks and a DELF B2 in French.
